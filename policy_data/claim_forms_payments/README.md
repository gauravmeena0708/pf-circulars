# Payment modes (NEFT, e-payment, CCPAP) and claim forms

Finance circulars (2009-2020) on how EPFO pays beneficiaries and on claim forms: the 2010 e-payment and NEFT implementation, dishonour of cheques (2010), fake-cheque precautions and limits (2011-2013), NEFT payments for EDLI benefits (2015-2016), the CCPAP / positive-pay module (2014, 2016), UAN-based withdrawal claim forms (2015), the Re 1 revenue-stamp dispensation (2015, 2017), composite claim forms for death cases (2017) and the 2017 claim-settlement SOP.

Stated 'immediate effect' and 'w.e.f.' dates are recorded as effective dates; follow-up circulars that only continue earlier ones are references. Five circulars carry their typed letter date (see the date notes).

Not covered: investment, pension payments to banks, VDR collection accounts, advances and bonus.
