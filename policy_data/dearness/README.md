# Dearness allowance / relief (staff and staff pensioners)

Central Government dearness allowance (DA) for EPFO staff and dearness relief (DR) for staff
pensioners, as conveyed by EPFO circulars from 2016 to April 2026. Thirty-nine documents,
38 relationships, two matters: **Dearness allowance (staff)** and **Dearness relief (staff
pensioners)**. This is not about EPF member benefits or EPS pensions.

- Each circular states "enhanced from the existing rate of A% to B% with effect from <date>".
  The effective date is recorded with that quote. The EPFO circular's issue date is later.
- Each rate partially modifies the previous one from its effective date; the earlier rate
  stands for earlier periods.
- 2016: DA/DR was 125% from 1 Jan 2016 (pre-7th CPC), then nil from 1 Jan 2016 and 2% from
  1 Jul 2016 under the revised pay structure.
- April 2020: DA/DR frozen at current rates (instalments of 1 Jan 2020, 1 Jul 2020 and
  1 Jan 2021 not paid). The July 2021 circulars subsume them: 17% to 28% from 1 Jul 2021, then
  28% to 31% also from 1 Jul 2021.
- Rates since: 34% (1 Jan 2022), 38%, 42%, 46%, 50%, 53%, 55%, 58% (1 Jul 2025), 60%
  (1 Jan 2026).
- The 1 Oct 2018 DR circular's rate is in the scanned image layer; its subject line is the
  checked text evidence (marked in `notes`).

Not covered: pre-2016 rates, allowance enhancements linked to DA, gratuity, CGHS, family
pension and pension revision. Originals were read from the issuer mirror on 3 Nov 2026
(the public site returned 404).
