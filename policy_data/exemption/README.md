# Exemption of establishments (grant, compliance audit, returns, surrender)

Compliance and Exemption cell circulars (2008-2026) on exemption and relaxation under section 17 of the EPF Act: grant and EDLI exemption proposals, compliance audit of exempted establishments, surcharge rates (2014, 2017), online returns of exempted and relaxed establishments (2014-2018), verification of closed establishments, cancellation and surrender, transfer of past accumulation and of securities on cancellation, the Pattern of Investment 2015 for exempted trusts and its amendments (2016, 2021), the Pension Division's EDLI exemption circulars (2017-2022), the 2023 SOP for grant, the 2023 SOP for management and regulation, the 2024 SOP and online system for surrender and the 2026 monthly review and amnesty circulars.

The three SOPs of 2023-2024 supersede the circulars listed in their annexures; those that are in this collection are linked. Other follow-up circulars are recorded as references or stated modifications. Three circulars carry their typed letter date (see the date notes).

Not covered: meeting minutes, RTI replies, court-case reports and Finance circulars on securities transfer.
