# Damages (14B), interest (7Q) and quasi-judicial proceedings (7A)

Compliance circulars (2007-2026) on the levy of damages under section 14B and interest under section 7Q (assessment, rates, relief during COVID, review of pending cases, return discontinued 2017), enquiries under section 7A (the 2007 arrangement of powers and its partial modifications of 2009 and 2012, the 2012-2016 quasi-judicial guidelines, 2017 jurisdiction, 2020 initiation guidelines, 2021 review of long-pending cases) and the 2025-2026 changes (section 14AC and 14B amendments in force from 29 October 2025, VISHWAS 2026 and the notification of authorised officers under the Code on Social Security, 2020).

Follow-up circulars are recorded as references; only the stated partial modifications are recorded as modifications. Five circulars carry their typed letter date (see the date notes).
