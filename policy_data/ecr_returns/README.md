# Employer returns, ECR, KYC and UAN activation

Compliance circulars (2012-2026) on employers' electronic returns and the Electronic Challan-cum-Return: the e-returns amendment (2012), Form 3A/6A updation, the 2016 UAN activation and KYC seeding drive, Aadhaar seeding in UAN (2018), the 2024-25 UAN activation and Aadhaar bank-seeding deadlines (extended repeatedly), the revamped ECR system for wage month September 2025 onwards and its FAQs, gross wages in the ECR from August 2025, the December 2025 discontinuation of the Aadhaar-seeding extension and the January 2026 due-date extensions. The 2018 abolition of the scanned PAN upload at registration is also included.

The deadline-extension chain is recorded as candidate links where earlier circulars are not named. Internal ABP/MIS returns are not included.
