# Recruitment rules and promotion

EPFO staff recruitment rules and regulations (notified and draft), DPC procedure and model
calendars, and MACP/NFU/NFSG guidance. Fifty-one documents, five relationships, three matters:
**Recruitment rules / regulations (by cadre)** (27), **DPC procedure and promotion guidelines**
(12) and **MACP / NFU / NFSG** (12). This is not about EPF members.

- Two supersessions are stated in the rules themselves and both predecessors are in the set:
  the 2017 Multi-Tasking Staff rules over the 2011 rules (GSR 457, 5 Dec 2017), and the 2019
  Section Supervisor rules over the 2017 regulations (Gazette of 31 Jan 2019).
- Fifteen documents are drafts placed on the website for stakeholder comments. They are
  consultation-only and never evidence of notified rules; most final versions are not here.
- Effective dates are recorded only where the circular states the Gazette date (PPS, MTS 2017,
  Section Supervisor 2017 and 2019).
- DPC model-calendar circulars of 2022 and 2023 are reminders (`references`), not new timelines.
- MACP, NFU and DPC circulars mostly forward DoPT or Department of Expenditure OMs, which govern.

Not covered: individual MACP grants, annual DPC notices, direct-recruitment appointment orders,
examinations, the 2025 Staff Regulations, and the RPFC Grade-II (Amendment) 2011 circular (not in
the mirror). Originals were read from the issuer mirror on 3 Nov 2026 (the public site returned 404);
every stated date was checked against the document header.
