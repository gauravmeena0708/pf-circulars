# IS systems, authentication and cyber security

Information Services circulars on the Issue Tracker and its replacement, mandatory two-factor and multi-factor authentication, the e-Office VPN, cyber security advisories, anti-piracy and forwarded Government IT policies. Sixteen documents.

- Issue Tracker: the 2018 web application, the January 2020 circular linking it to CPGRAMS and RTI, and the 23 July 2026 circular deploying Samadhan Setu and stating that the Issue Tracker portal is discontinued (a verified supersession with a stated effective date).
- Authentication: two-factor (2020) and multi-factor (2021, with immediate effect) for e-mail, the 2024 web VPN with MFA and the 2025 VPN password change.
- Cyber security: 2022 advisories, 2016 cyber-incident reporting and anti-piracy circulars. Advisories are alerts, not standing rules.

Two circulars carry their typed letter date instead of the issuer listing date (see the date notes).
