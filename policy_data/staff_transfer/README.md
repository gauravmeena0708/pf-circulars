# EPFO staff transfer policy

Cadre transfer policies for EPFO staff and their amendments, 2022 to 2025. Sixteen
documents, three matters (Commissioners' cadre, Group B, Group C/SSA) and eleven
relationships. This concerns staff postings, not EPF member account transfers.

- Drafts circulated for comments (Feb 2022 Commissioners and Group B, May 2022 SSA) are
  `consultation` documents and never supply operative evidence.
- Commissioners: the 30 March 2022 policy is amended only in Paras 3/3.3 (30 May 2022)
  and 5.18(iv) (25 Jan 2024). The 2025 policy and its 21 Feb 2025 corrigendum follow.
- Group B: the 14 June 2022 policy, the intra-state amendment (23 Dec 2022) and the GB-TC
  clarification (31 Jan 2023), then the 2025 policy.
- Group C/SSA: the 12 Dec 2022 policy, the 29 Jan 2024 circular recording CAT stays against
  implementing it, then the 2025 Group C policy and its 25 Nov 2025 modifications.
- Each 2025 policy says it supersedes "earlier policies/existing guidelines" without naming
  them; those links stay `target_unverified`.
- The Group C clarification is dated 25 November 2025 on the document. The issuer's website
  listing (and the earlier search index) showed 25 April 2025; the index was corrected.
- Not covered: yearly Annual General Transfer option notices, vacancy/block-post circulars,
  individual orders, 2015-2020 policies, Official Language and Ministry cadre policies.

Originals are read from the issuer mirror (public site returned 404 on 3 Nov 2026); hashes
in `sources/` record those bytes.
