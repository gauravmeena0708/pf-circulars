# EPFO employee benefits collection

Choose **EPFO employee benefits** under Policy collection and select a matter,
or ask a question naming briefcase, laptop, telephone/mobile bills, newspaper
reimbursement or staff medical reimbursement. Circular history has the same matter
filter and issue-date cutoff. This collection concerns EPFO staff, not EPF members.

24 catalogue entries cover five matters. Six originals have checked physical-page
text. Remaining originals are explicitly unverified and can only receive
supplementary hybrid evidence with unverified applicability and date controls.

The recovered 15 October 2024 briefcase order expressly supersedes both the
1 October 2024 and 9 December 2022 orders. Both October tables were visually checked
and transcribed where OCR lost cells. The invoice period, cadre/equivalence and
complete conditions must be read alongside each ceiling; pay level alone does not
establish entitlement. Original PDFs and raw extraction are preserved.

The 18 December 2024 device letter is separate from its enclosed July 2023 DoE OM.
An enclosed OM's issue date is not assumed to establish EPFO adoption from that date.
The newspaper and telephone letters are dated 17 April 2018; later website posting
dates are not used as their issue dates.

Medical/CGHS/CS(MA)/fixed medical allowance and the older driver mobile-bill circular
remain source gaps. Their catalogue entries do not verify amounts, eligibility,
current rules or sanction authority. Broader entitlement or medical decisions need
the missing originals and any later instructions/judicial directions.
