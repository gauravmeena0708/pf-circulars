# APAR / vigilance clearance

EPFO staff appraisal (APAR) timelines on SPARROW and vigilance clearance guidelines.
Twenty-two documents, sixteen relationships, four matters: APAR system, APAR 2024-25
timelines, APAR 2025-26 timelines and vigilance clearance.

- The 2024-25 cycle is a single chain: the 26 Aug 2025 circular, a 22 Sep 2025 revision, a
  1 Oct 2025 corrigendum (21/31 January typed 2025, meant 2026) and ten further revisions to
  25 Mar 2026, then a 2 Jun 2026 revision of the representation stage. Each revision
  changes the dates for the listed activities only (`partially_modifies`).
- The 2025-26 cycle starts 20 Apr 2026 with revisions on 16 Jun, 16 Jul and 13 Aug 2026.
- As of a date, the latest circular issued on or before it states the operative dates for
  the activities it lists. This history does not compute deadlines.
- Vigilance clearance: the January 2017 RPFC-I rule for Group C/D, the September 2017
  clarification for RPFC-II-headed offices and the October 2022 revised proforma. The 2014
  office order the proforma replaces and the 2015/April 2017 letters are not in the set.
- Not covered: APAR cycles outside 2020-21 and 2024-26, DoPT rules, individual clearance
  requests.
