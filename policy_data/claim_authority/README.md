# Claim settlement authority

Who may settle or authorise EPF claims and where claim work is processed. Twelve documents,
three relationships, four matters: **Claim settlement: financial powers**, **Inoperative
accounts**, **Multi-location settlement and SSO claim processing** and **Name-correction
powers (2016-17)**. It is not the procedure for filing a claim.

- Financial powers for claim settlement (Section Supervisors, APFCs/Accounts Officers, closed
  establishments, exceptional transactions: 2012, 2017, 2021, 2025) were moved here from the
  administrative and financial powers collection, unchanged.
- Inoperative accounts: the March 2011 circular delegates authorisation of claims out of
  inoperative accounts to Accounts Officers up to a limit; the July 2014 circular changes who may
  settle and the limits (citing the 2011 circular and the March 2012 Section Supervisor
  delegation by number).
- MLC/CRO: the July 2021 circular delegates assigning a Collaborating Regional Office under
  Multi-Location Claim Settlement to Zonal ACCs. The October 2023 circular records delegation of
  claim settlement to Special State Offices (Executive Committee approval of 10 March 2023).
- Name correction: the 2016 and 2017 delegations are kept here; the later JD process is in the JD
  policies.

Originals marked `compiled_copy` were read from a compilation of circulars, not a public file.
