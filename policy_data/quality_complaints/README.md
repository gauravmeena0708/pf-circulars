# ISO 9001 and vigilance complaint procedures

Two small sets of Headquarters circulars: the 2012-2015 push to implement ISO 9001 certification in field offices (the 9 November 2012 letter and reminders), and Vigilance circulars on complaints (the 2012 Public Interest Disclosure procedure, the 2014 instruction to record complainants' mobile numbers and e-mail IDs, and the December 2014 streamlining of the investigation mechanism, in force with immediate effect).

Reminders are recorded as references to the original letter. The ISO programme's outcome is not stated in any circular here.

Not covered: public grievance redressal (CPGRAMS and EPFiGMS).
