# Panel advocates: fees and empanelment

EPFO panel advocate fee revisions and empanelment circulars. Seven documents, three
relationships, two matters: **Panel advocate fees** and **Empanelment of advocates**.

- January 2012: offices told to regulate fees under the Law Ministry's revised structure
  "with effect from 1.9.2011" (the enclosed OM is dated 18.09.2011 and says "immediate
  effect"; the circular's wording is recorded). July 2012 clarifies fees for EPFAT, High
  Court and CAT counsel.
- January 2017: new fee tables A-D. July 2026: revised tables after the Law Ministry OM of
  05.02.2026, applicable from the circular's issue date (8 July 2026).
- 2017 empanelment: eligibility raised to ten years; after the EPFAT merger into the CGITs
  (Gazette of 26 May 2017) advocates are drawn from High Court panels or fresh local panels.
- The fee-to-fee links are candidate links (target unverified): neither the 2017 nor the
  2026 circular names the earlier EPFO circular.

Not included: the 29 July 2026 order listing named advocates, zonal notices and RTI
replies. Fee amounts are in the originals' tables. Originals were read from the issuer
mirror on 3 Nov 2026 (the public site returned 404).
