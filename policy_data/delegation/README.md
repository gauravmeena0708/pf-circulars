# Matter-specific delegation history

Choose **Delegation of powers** and a matter in research or Circular history.

member entitlements and staff allowances retain their separate collections.

PDNASS/ZTIs, medical sanctions, personnel/appointments, inquiry/presenting-officer honoraria,
staff housing and publicity. 33 entries have checked physical-page sources; 24 of them were
upgraded from catalogue-only entries using pages of a compiled copy of the circulars (marked
`compiled_copy`; no public download is offered for those). Missing originals remain visible.
More recent does not mean applicable.
