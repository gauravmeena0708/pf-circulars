# Pension member rules: Para 11(3), Para 12, date of birth and Aadhaar

Pension Division circulars (2006-2024): contribution on salary above the statutory limit under Para 11(3) of EPS 1995 (2013-2014), Para 12 pension calculation and the 2008 notification, two years' weightage of service for members with 20 or more years (2016-2017), the 2006 comprehensive guidelines on change of date of birth and their 2017 follow-ups, Aadhaar as identity document with pension claims and the Aadhaar pension claim form (2017-2018), higher-wage pension (2018, 2023) and the 2024 modification of Table B.

Follow-up circulars are recorded as references or clarifications; none replaces the earlier ones. Two circulars carry their typed letter date (see the date notes). Jeevan Pramaan life certificates are not included.
