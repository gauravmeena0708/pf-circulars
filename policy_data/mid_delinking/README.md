# Mid Delinking

- De-linking a wrong MID is separate from correcting Aadhaar, merging genuine service, transferring balances or correcting EPS contributions.
- The 2026 circular says separate instructions will follow for transfer of de-linked balances to SRF; no automatic balance-transfer outcome is inferred.
- Both full manuals are extracted, but screenshots and OCR may contain errors. Cover instructions and manual validation tables must be reconciled; no date-of-joining cutoff is certified here.
- January manual version 1.0 dated 11 January is distinct from the circular issue date of 17 January.
- 2026 original URL is unavailable; a mirror copy is retained, with issuer-hosted byte identity unverified.
- Issue-date filtering does not establish operational availability or legal commencement.
