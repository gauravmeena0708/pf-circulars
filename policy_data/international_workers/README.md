# International workers, Certificates of Coverage and Social Security Agreements

Pension Division and International Workers Unit circulars (2008-2026): the 2008 and 2010 scheme amendments for international workers, the January 2009 guidelines, the 2011-2012 compliance and clarification circulars, Certificate of Coverage guidelines and software (2013-2014), the Social Security Agreement implementation circulars (Switzerland 2011, Sweden 2014, Norway 2015, Austria 2015, Canada 2015, Australia 2016, Brazil 2024), the Singapore CECA exemption circulars, the June 2017 definition circular and the 2014 and March 2026 circulars on payment to overseas bank accounts.

Effective dates are recorded where a circular states them (for example the dates on which each agreement entered into force). Three circulars carry their typed letter date (see the date notes).
