# Foreign visit permissions

EPFO staff permission for private foreign visits and who may grant it. Five documents, two
relationships.

- 31 Oct 2013: only the CPFC holds the delegated power; permissions were being granted at lower
  levels. 10 Oct 2014: the CPFC delegates Group C permission to ACC (HQ)-HR.
- 2 May 2016: prior permission for leaving the station for going abroad on private visit, and
  delegation thereof; the 6 Oct 2017 delegation supersedes it (verified link).
- 8 Apr 2026: vigilance clearance for Group C foreign-visit permission under the 2025 Staff
  Regulations; it cites the 2017 delegation and is not stated to replace it.

Conditions come from the DoPT OM cited in the circulars and are not restated. Originals marked
`compiled_copy` were read from a compilation of circulars, not a public file.
