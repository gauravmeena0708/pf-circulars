# Handling of legal cases in field offices

Compliance and Legal Cell circulars (2014-2025) on how field offices handle court cases: the 16 May 2014 comprehensive guidelines and the circulars that reiterate or add to them (2015, 2016 consumer forums, 2017), analysis of adverse orders (2015), the Legal Dashboard data-entry instructions (2015-2016), the nodal office for cases of other regional offices (2018), service-matter cases (2019), the 2022 Task Force and the 2025 Strengthening Litigation Management circular.

Later circulars reiterate the 2014 guidelines and are recorded as references; none is stated to replace them. Five circulars carry their typed letter date instead of the issuer listing date (see the date notes).

Not covered: panel advocates (see the Panel advocates collection), forwarded judgments and case-specific orders.
