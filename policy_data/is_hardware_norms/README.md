# IT hardware specifications and norms

Information Services specifications and norms for EPFO computer hardware: the 2008 thin-client decision and obsolescence policy, the 2009-2018 norms for offices, the 2017 norms for mobile devices, the 2018 laptop purchase guidelines, and the 2019 and 2025 specifications of laptops, desktops and tablets.

- The 2019 circular supersedes all earlier circulars on laptop, desktop, printer, UPS, virus-scanner and MS Office norms and specifications (generic wording; links unverified).
- The 11 December 2025 specifications supersede the 25 October 2021 ones (not in the set) and are in force immediately until further orders (stated).
- The obsolescence policy of 5 November 2008 is still cited as binding in December 2018 and January 2019.

Not covered: AMC and warranty circulars, purchase approvals, staff laptop entitlement (Employee benefits) and delegation of purchase powers (Delegation).
