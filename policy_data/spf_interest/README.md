# Staff Provident Fund interest rate

HRD circulars (2012 onward) announcing the interest rate credited to the Staff Provident Fund accumulations of EPFO employees. The rate follows the Government of India General Provident Fund rate, announced for each financial year or quarter.

Each notice is dated and keeps its own period; they are not treated as replacing one another. The series has gaps; see the limitations. Three notices carry their typed letter date (see the date notes).

Not covered: the EPF member interest rate (see the EPF interest rate collection) and Staff Provident Fund withdrawals or advances.
